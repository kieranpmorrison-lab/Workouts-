Progressive Overload

Add weight — when all sets hit top of rep range with good form

Add reps — if you can't go heavier, add 1-2 reps/set

Add a set — Phase 2-3 only, for lagging areas

Reduce rest — for isolation/arm work (90s → 60s)

Compounds: +2.5-5 lbs/week
Isolations: +2.5 lbs every 1-2 weeks

Running Plan by Phase

Phase 1 (Wk 1-4): Run/walk intervals — 5 min warmup, 8 rounds of 2 min jog / 1 min walk, 5 min cooldown (~35 min)

Phase 2 (Wk 5-8): Continuous running build — 5 min warmup, 20-25 min continuous jog (conversational pace), 5 min cooldown. Add 2-3 min/week.

Phase 3 (Wk 9-12): Endurance + tempo — Wk 9-10: 25-30 min easy. Wk 11-12: 10 min easy, 10-12 min tempo, 5 min easy cooldown.

Incline Walk Guidelines

Settings: 10-15% incline, 3.0-3.5 mph, 30-45 min

Target HR: 120-140 bpm (Zone 2 — nasal breathing, conversational)

Don't hold the rails — engage core, pump arms

Progression: +0.5-1% incline every 2 weeks

Yoga

When: Wednesday (alternate with incline walk) or Sunday

Type: Vinyasa or Power Yoga preferred (builds stability through range)

Note: With hypermobility, focus on engaging muscles during poses rather than sinking into end range.

Knee Considerations

  • Leg press over deep barbell squats if knees flare
  • Hack squat = fixed path, less knee shear
  • Avoid: full-depth pistol squats, heavy short-ROM extensions, box jumps
  • Running: swap for bike/elliptical if knees protest
  • Never lock out joints — maintain slight bend at top

Hypermobility Notes

  • Don't sink into end range on any movement
  • Focus on muscle engagement, not joint stretch
  • Vinyasa yoga > Yin yoga (builds control through range)
  • Tempo work is your friend — keeps muscles under tension

Nutrition (Brief)

  • Protein: 1g per lb bodyweight, 4+ meals/day
  • Slight deficit on rest/cardio days
  • Maintenance/surplus on lift days
  • Sleep: 7-8 hrs minimum
  • Hydration: 0.5-1 oz per lb bodyweight
  • Protein within 1-2 hrs post-workout

Recovery Signals

  • Joint pain above a 3/10 → modify or skip
  • Sleep quality dropping → deload week
  • Consistent fatigue 3+ days → check nutrition
  • If you miss a day, don't double up — just continue

Arm Growth Tips

  • ~20 direct arm sets/week by Phase 3
  • Slow eccentrics (3-4 sec negatives) for biceps
  • Full stretch at bottom of curls
  • Squeeze at peak contraction on every rep
  • Vary grip: hammer, supinated, reverse

Ab Training Philosophy

  • 4x/week built into sessions as finishers
  • Mix: anti-rotation (Pallof), flexion (crunches), isometric (planks)
  • Progressive overload applies to abs too
  • Add weight/reps every 2 weeks
  • Brace hard — don't just go through motions
